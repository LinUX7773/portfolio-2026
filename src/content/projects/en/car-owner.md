## My key takeaways

In this project, I got valuable feedback from user-testing. I used a combination of interview and test script, emphasising flow and a deeper understanding of pain points. It was amazing to see the users go from enjoying themselves to utter frustration, talking about their pain — arms flying. I learned that a combination like this can save time, be more fun and give better and truer outcomes!

## Setting project goals

For this project, I wanted to encourage the company to set goals, and so we did a SMART goal workshop. We started with a template with different facets and examples to draw inspiration from. We chose 5 good parameters, but after looking at our prioritised user groups, ditched 3 of them, and ended up with 2 clear SMART goals. This product just launched, and we're looking forward to seeing how it performs against our goals. It was a fun experience working with 1881, a private company that's focused and goal-oriented.

## My role, process and method

1881 already has a skilled Web Designer on board, and so they hired me for the extra 2 hands on deck. I helped set goals and agree on prioritised user groups and features. This project had already had a pre-project with thorough user research, so I just filled in the blanks. I did an initial interview round with users who already use this type of product.

## Challenges

My biggest challenge with this project was to understand the market. I did some market research and was presented with several findings, but it was still difficult to understand how many visits and sales we should expect. It's been a minute since I last worked with sales on a B2C product like this, and it was exhilarating to do it again!
