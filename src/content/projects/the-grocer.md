## My key takeaways

The customers represented a variety of segments and appeared quite diverse at first glance. However, upon conducting a comprehensive analysis, we discovered that the majority of users shared similar values, objectives, and requirements for the tool.

## Project goals

- Develop a new bulk food ordering tool for 40% of Norway's grocery shops
- Simplify onboarding process, eliminating the need for extensive onsite training

## Research and development process

### Initial research

- Conducted comprehensive interviews with shopkeepers and employees
- Identified common values and needs across diverse shop profiles

### Design and strategy

- Facilitated ideation workshops for functionality
- Led product strategy, KPIs, roadmap, and user-story development
- Collaborated closely with a skilled UI designer, enhancing mutual expertise

## Challenges and solutions

### Team composition

- 90% consultant-based team with limited industry-specific knowledge
- Leveraged customer willingness to provide insights and support

### Stakeholder management

- Navigated distributed ownership across departments
- Overcame initial delays by ensuring consistent stakeholder involvement

## Project outcomes

- Successful implementation
- Increased efficiency, allowing shopkeepers more time for customer and employee interaction
- Positive user feedback on the tool's impact on daily operations

This e-commerce solution has significantly improved the bulk ordering process for Norwegian grocers, streamlining their operations and enhancing their ability to focus on core business activities.
